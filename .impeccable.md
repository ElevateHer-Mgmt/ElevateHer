# Design Context — Elevate Her

Source: `brand/agence.md`, `brand/charte.md`, `brand/references.md` (Ethan's own brand documents). Those files win over this summary.

## Target audience
Ambitious female content creators (young, mostly arriving from Instagram and TikTok on mobile) who want to turn their personal brand into a lasting business. Secondary: brands, partners and press.

## Use cases
- Understand in seconds what Elevate Her is: a selective creator agency backed by a dedicated team.
- Feel the selectivity (Private Club, limited roster, by application only).
- Apply through the embedded form: "Apply to Elevate Her" is the only primary action.

## Brand personality / tone
Feminine, distinguished, young, premium, selective. Elegant without being cold, chic without being dated. A fashion magazine for a generation of ambitious creators, with the discretion of a private club. The creator is always the subject; the team stays behind the scenes.

## Imposed identity (non negotiable)
- Colours: cream #FFF8F0 (background), ink #1B1B1B (text), powder rose #D9A3A5 (signature, decorative only, never text), accent rose #B97A80 (italic words in large headings only, 32px+), pale rose #EABDBD (section backgrounds), deep rose #9E5C63 (links, buttons with white text, small rose text), taupe #5C5452 (secondary text), white #FFFFFF (cards).
- Fonts: Playfair (variable, optical size) 400–500 for headings, 300 for body (min 18px desktop / 17px mobile, line-height ~1.6); Jost 400–500 uppercase, letter-spacing 0.2–0.3em for labels, menus, buttons. Nothing else.
- Logo: `brand/logo/elevate-her-wordmark.svg` only, never rebuilt with a font. Round badge for favicon, share image and decorative seal.
- Motifs: four-point star ✦ sparingly, thin powder-rose arcs and circles (echo of the badge), silky pale-rose drapes and soft palm-leaf shadows for backgrounds, lots of white space, soft rounded corners, very light shadows.

## Avoid
Candy pink, fuchsia, loud gradients, cheap glitter, pure black in large areas, any suggestive imagery, any template look, any "by women, for women" phrasing.

## Signature idea to explore
The path Brand → Audience → Growth → Revenue → Opportunities as the site's signature element, e.g. a scroll-driven section.
