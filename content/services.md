## 2. Le parcours, chapitre par chapitre

### 01 · Brand (#brand)

**H2** : Brand. Start with who you *are*.

**Texte** : Everything that comes later depends on this step. We look at what your brand stands for, who it speaks to and where it's going, then we write a strategy for you alone. Not a framework applied to everyone. Yours.

That strategy becomes the reference for every decision after it: what you post, what you say yes to, what you turn down.

**Included** :
- **Brand development** — Positioning, identity and consistency across everything you publish.
- **Personalised strategy** — A written plan for your brand and your goals, revisited as you grow.

**Outcome** : You know exactly what your brand is. So does everyone who sees it.

### 02 · Audience (#audience)

**H2** : Audience. Reach the people who should know *you*.

**Texte** : A brand needs to be seen, consistently, without you burning out. We plan your content around your rhythm and your voice, then manage your social presence day to day so your time goes into creating.

**Included** :
- **Individual content planning** — A content calendar built for you: themes, formats, timing.
- **Social media management** — Your accounts handled every day: publishing, presence, consistency.

**Outcome** : Your audience hears from you regularly, and it always sounds like you.

### 03 · Growth (#growth)

**H2** : Growth. Momentum, with a team behind *it*.

**Texte** : Growth isn't a campaign. It's a routine. Your dedicated team follows your numbers, sees what works, adjusts the plan and tells you plainly what comes next. You're not guessing alone.

**Included** :
- **A dedicated team** — People who know your account and your goals, assigned to you.
- **Fast communication** — Short loops. Questions get answers, decisions get made.

**Outcome** : Steady progress instead of bursts and silences.

### 04 · Revenue (#revenue)

**H2** : Revenue. Turn attention into *income*.

**Texte** : An audience is worth building. It's worth more when it pays. Revenue management structures your income around more than one source, follows it closely and grows it with your brand, so money stops being an afterthought.

**Included** :
- **Revenue management** — Your income streams structured, tracked and developed.

**Outcome** : Several sources of income, managed. Not one you hope will hold.

### 05 · Opportunities (#opportunities)

**H2** : Opportunities. Open the doors that fit your *brand*.

**Texte** : The right partnership at the right moment can change a year. The wrong one can cost you trust. We develop brand partnerships for you: the right partners, approached the right way, negotiated on terms that respect your image.

**Included** :
- **Brand partnership development** — Partners sourced, approached and negotiated for you.

**Outcome** : Partnerships that build your brand instead of diluting it.
