## 4. FAQ

**Eyebrow** : QUESTIONS

**H2** : Before you *apply*.

**1. Who can apply?**
Any creator who's serious about turning her personal brand into a lasting business. We work with ambitious female creators across niches. If you're not sure you qualify, apply anyway. The conversation is where we find out.

**2. Do I need a certain audience size?**
We look at the whole picture: your brand, your consistency, your ambition and where you want to go. Numbers are part of it. They're not the whole story. [TO COMPLETE : critère minimum éventuel, à confirmer par Ethan]

**3. What does "Private Club" mean?**
That we're selective. We take on a limited number of creators so the team can stay deeply involved in every account. It's a roster you're chosen for, not a list you join.

**4. What does "Her" mean?**
Her is who we build for: ambitious female creators. The name says who the work is for, nothing more.

**5. Do I stay in control of my brand and my accounts?**
Yes. You remain the face and the owner of your brand. The team works behind the scenes, with you, never instead of you. Account protection and content protection are part of the service.

**6. What happens after I apply, and how long does it take?**
The team reads your application and gets back to you within [TO COMPLETE : délai]. If there's a fit, we set up a call. If we're not the right fit, we tell you so, clearly.
